Who we are

More than an event.
A gathering.

Sisters Squad is a Kolhapur-based collective creating spaces where women can dress up, show up, and simply be — surrounded by music, colour, and each other. Every evening we host is designed to feel exclusive, warm, and unforgettable.

01

Crafted for you

Every detail — from the venue to the meal to the music — is chosen with our audience in mind. No corners cut.

02

Only for women

Our events are exclusively for women, creating a safe and celebratory space to let go and be yourself.

03

Rooted in Kolhapur

Local traditions, local artists, local flavour — our events celebrate the culture we call home.
